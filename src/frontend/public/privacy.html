Privacy policy

Last updated 27 September 2026

  • We use Google sign-in only to save your sets to your account.
  • Your sets are private unless you publish them to the gallery.
  • We send your descriptions and uploaded images to AI services to design your set.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.
  • Email tobyscoxsc@gmail.com to delete your account or ask a question.

Who we are

Legolizer (www.legolizer.tech) turns a description or a picture into a LEGO-style model with a parts list and building instructions. For anything about this policy or your data, email tobyscoxsc@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics, when you sign in with Google: your Google account ID, email address, first name, and a link to your profile photo. We never see your Google password or any other Google data.
  • What you create: set names, descriptions, images you upload or take with your camera, the changes you ask for when refining a set, and the sets we make for you (3D model files, parts lists, instructions, renders, and the working files used to design them).
  • A sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days, or until you sign out. We store only a scrambled (hashed) copy of it.
  • A note in your browser of the last set you opened, so it reopens next time. This stays on your device.
  • Standard request logs kept by our hosting providers, such as IP address and browser type, used for security and keeping the site running.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your sets in your account, on any device you sign in from.
  • To design and build the sets you ask for.
  • To show sets you choose to publish in the public gallery.
  • To keep the service working and fair, for example by limiting each person to one set in progress at a time.

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Services that handle your data

  • Google handles sign-in. Pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.
  • Vercel hosts the website and its API.
  • Amazon Web Services (United States) stores accounts and sets and runs the machines that build them.
  • OpenAI and xAI receive your descriptions, uploaded images, and requested changes, to draw a concept image and design your set.
  • Wikipedia: for well-known real-world subjects, our servers search Wikipedia for a freely licensed reference photo. Only the subject is searched for, never anything about you.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy.

What other people can see

Your sets are private: other people cannot list or open them. If you choose Publish to gallery, anyone can see that set's name, description and expanded prompt, its colors and reference-photo credit, the change you asked for (for refined sets), the 3D model, parts list, instructions, and render, and your first name as its author.

Your email address, profile photo, and uploaded images are never shown to other people. Choose Remove from gallery at any time to make a set private again. File links that someone already opened can keep working for up to 15 minutes.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and sets until you ask us to delete them. The temporary copy of an uploaded image is deleted after 30 days; a private copy stays with the set it was used for. To delete your account and everything in it, email tobyscoxsc@gmail.com from the address you sign in with. We will delete it within 30 days.

Children

Legolizer is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child under 13 has signed in, email us and we will delete their account.

Security

The site uses HTTPS, your files are stored privately, and only the service itself can read them. No online service is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect to what the service needs.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. For significant changes, we will also post a notice on the site.
